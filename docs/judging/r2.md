# Judging release r1.5: pre-registered rule r2

Registered 2026-10-28, 8 hours into release r1.5's record (it began with issue 498095, 2026-10-27 23:00 UTC), before
any result could support a claim. From the first issue sealed after this file was merged, every manifest carries this
file's keccak256 hash. The rule is never changed: a revision would be a new file with its own start issue. Rule r1
stays in force as written.

## The question
Does EV-GBM, a learned correction on top of a calendar-aware classical model, forecast the 1- and 4-hour questions
better than:
- **(A)** the classical volatility models GARCH and HAR, and
- **(B)** EV-HAR, the classical model that EV-GBM corrects?

Claim A asks whether the forecaster beats the classical bar. Claim B asks whether the learning is the reason: EV-HAR
already knows the US session, weekends and scheduled releases, and only the trees separate the two.

- **Judged:** EV-GBM, `ev-gbm-r15`, a program with a parameter file
  (`0xb968a022ac9be499bdd06a67a764ee53bdb38c606c7f8e1fc2748f1972845454`; see
  [`engine/releases/r1.5`](../../engine/releases/r1.5/release.json)).
- **Opponents:**
  - claim A: `garch-r15` (`0xb15791e0ca9a42ca356baf961ad1b7af87f07a28ab37ce5762731453a5f7f767`) and `har-r15`
    (`0x9b05a423a031ced018bba945226540dc3edd8dc75d00e9599a3436fa1791e8a5`);
  - claim B: `ev-har-r15` (`0xdd7e3581c3acac711476e40ae561fd76c19bcc19a55761878249e915ec290c3c`).
- **Why opponents of release r1.5:** all four were fitted on the same data, 2025-02-01 to 2026-09-27, exactly as the
  rolling test fits them; the trees were stopped on the month after, to 2026-10-27. A model fitted on older data
  loses about 0.002 nats a month against a refit ([the research note](../research/2026-10-27-ev-gbm.md)), so
  opponents fitted earlier would give the judged model an edge it did not learn.
- **Why these questions:** in a rolling-origin test of the same procedure (seven monthly folds, every hour scored),
  EV-GBM beat EV-HAR (+0.0150 nats per question) and GARCH (+0.0317) at 1 and 4 hours in all seven months, with
  both pooled intervals clear of zero, and was unresolved against EV-HAR at 24 hours
  ([the note](../research/2026-10-27-ev-gbm.md)). This rule tests that on fresh, pre-committed data.

## The statistic
As in rule r1:
- **Score:** the binned log score of each committed forecast (basis points / 10,000) on its realized bin.
- **Per issue:** the mean, over the seven assets and the 1- and 4-hour horizons, of EV-GBM's score minus the
  opponent's. An issue counts once both horizons have settled and both forecasters forecast all 14 of its questions.
  Issues where either one is missing are left out and counted. Nothing else is left out, re-weighted or restarted.
- **Gain:** the mean of the per-issue values, each issue weighted equally.
- **Uncertainty:** a circular block bootstrap over issues in order (2,000 resamples, seed 0), with blocks of 72
  issues and, as a check, 168 issues. z is the gain divided by the standard deviation of the resampled gains.

## The decision
- Three scheduled looks. Look k uses the issues of the first d days of r1.5's record (issues 498095 to
  498095 + 24·d − 1), once their 4-hour questions have settled:

  | look | d | uses issues up to | computed after (UTC) | EV-GBM beats the opponent if |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | 1 | 90 | 500254 | 2027-01-27 00:00 | z ≥ 4.36 |
  | 2 | 180 | 502414 | 2027-04-27 00:00 | z ≥ 2.99 |
  | 3 | 365 | 506854 | 2027-10-29 00:00 | z ≥ 1.97 |

- A claim needs z above the threshold with both block lengths. The thresholds spend a one-sided 2.5% error rate per
  opponent across the three looks (Lan-DeMets, O'Brien-Fleming-type spending at information fractions 90/365,
  180/365 and 1).
- **Claim A** needs a claim against both GARCH and HAR. **Claim B** needs a claim against EV-HAR. Each opponent is a
  one-sided 2.5% test. The two claims are reported separately; neither is pooled with the other or with rule r1.
- Once a claim is made, it stands. Later looks are still reported. If no look reaches its threshold, the result is
  "no evidence that EV-GBM beats" that opponent on these questions.
- The mirror claim (the opponent beats EV-GBM) uses the same thresholds on −z.

## What is published, and when
- **Each look:** the gain, both z values, the number of issues used and left out, and the verdict, whatever it says.
- **Day 30** (issues up to 498814, after 2026-11-28 00:00 UTC): a direction report with the same numbers and no verdict.
- **Between looks:** the running statistic appears on the scoreboard labelled "running, unresolved".
- **Reported but never claimed:** the 24-hour questions, all horizons together, each horizon alone, EV-GBM against
  r1's GARCH and HAR and r1.3's EV-HAR, and the instance's age effect (gain by month of the record).
- **Out of scope:** later refits (a release fitted on newer data is a different forecaster), other forecasters, and
  pools. They are exploratory until they get a rule of their own.
