# EV-GBM, and how fast a fitted model fades

2026-10-27 · development evidence (nothing was selected on these folds; the live record is the test)

## Question
EV-NET, a small network on top of EV-HAR, beat EV-HAR in every month of a rolling-origin test
([the learned-layer note](2026-10-27-learned-layer.md)). An exploratory LightGBM model did about twice as well.
Does a boosted-tree layer, built and frozen the way a release ships it, hold up in the same test? And since a
release freezes one fit for months, how fast does a fit fade?

## The model: EV-GBM
EV-GBM keeps EV-HAR's distribution and adds boosted trees to its log-probabilities, one LightGBM model per horizon
(`laplace_research.evolve.gbm`):

    logits = log EV-HAR(bins) + per bin, the sum of its trees' leaves

- **The loss.** Multiclass log loss over the ten bins, which is exactly the binned log score. Boosting starts from
  EV-HAR's log-probabilities, so the trees only learn where EV-HAR is wrong.
- **The inputs.** EV-NET's 27 inputs, plus the asset's index and the raw UTC hour and weekday.
- **The settings.** Fixed before the test: learning rate 0.03, 15 leaves, at least 400 rows per leaf, L2 10, 80%
  row and column sampling, deterministic. Boosting stops at the round that scores best on a later window, and keeps
  no trees at all if none beats EV-HAR there.

**Frozen.** The trees are exported to flat arrays, and every forecast walks those arrays with numpy, research
included. The exported trees match LightGBM's own predictions to 1e-12. The arrays are too large for a program's
16 KB, so they ship in a parameter file: public arrays in canonical JSON, read by the program as `ctx.params`. The
program's identity commits to its source and to that file. The program shares EV-NET's code for EV-HAR and the
inputs, character for character. Given the same EV-HAR spread, its inputs and tree sums are research's exactly.

## The test: rolling origin, as before
These are the same seven monthly folds as the learned-layer note (April to October 2026, 4,784 test hours).
- **Fitting.** Every model is fitted on every hour from 2025-02-01 to the month before the test month.
- **Stopping.** The learned layers stop on that month.
- **Scoring.** Every hour of the test month is scored.

Gains are per forecast hour, averaged over the seven assets, in nats per question, with 95% intervals from a
circular block bootstrap over 72-hour blocks. Reproduce with `laplace-research evolve rolling --run-id senses2 --ages`
(the `gbm` extra). Research now computes returns as log price ratios, as programs do (they had been sums of
hourly returns, off by 1e-15 where a price came back to the same value); EV-HAR and GARCH reproduce the earlier
note exactly, and EV-NET's pooled gain is unchanged.

- **EV-GBM beats EV-HAR in every month,** by +0.015 at 1 and 4 hours, nearly twice EV-NET's +0.008. It gains at 4
  hours too, where the network gained nothing, and it does no harm at 24 hours.

- **The scratch comparison** in the learned-layer note had +0.0166. This build differs in its inputs (EV-NET's
  cyclic clock, the asset as a number rather than a category) and lands within its interval.

## How fast a fit fades
A release freezes one fit, while the test above refits every month. With `--ages`, each fold's models also score
every later month, and the tables below read them by the number of months since the fit.

**Stale minus fresh.** On the same test month, a model fitted k months earlier against the same model refitted for
that month, at 1h + 4h:

| months since the fit | GARCH | EV-HAR | EV-NET | EV-GBM |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | -0.0006 | -0.0020 | +0.0013 | -0.0014 |
| 2 | -0.0032 | -0.0040 | -0.0019 | -0.0056 |
| 3 | -0.0071 | -0.0056 | -0.0086 | -0.0083 |
| 4 | -0.0059 | -0.0044 | -0.0038 | -0.0078 |
| 5 | +0.0006 | -0.0097 | -0.0067 | -0.0108 |
| 6 (one month) | -0.0020 | -0.0054 | -0.0197 | -0.0154 |

**The same fit.** Each learned layer against the EV-HAR fitted with it, by months since the fit, at 1h + 4h:

| months since the fit | EV-GBM vs EV-HAR | EV-NET vs EV-HAR |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | +0.0150 (+0.0098 to +0.0206) | +0.0084 (+0.0039 to +0.0136) |
| 1 | +0.0145 (+0.0096 to +0.0190) | +0.0107 (+0.0062 to +0.0150) |
| 2 | +0.0118 (+0.0056 to +0.0177) | +0.0090 (+0.0033 to +0.0147) |
| 3 | +0.0123 (+0.0063 to +0.0187) | +0.0050 (-0.0004 to +0.0107) |
| 4 | +0.0126 (+0.0042 to +0.0217) | +0.0091 (+0.0005 to +0.0182) |
| 5 | +0.0077 (-0.0022 to +0.0177) | +0.0084 (-0.0037 to +0.0207) |
| 6 (one month) | -0.0017 (-0.0150 to +0.0123) | -0.0099 (-0.0261 to +0.0064) |

- **Every model fades,** by about 0.002 nats a month for the first few months: the classical models about as fast
  as the learned layers.
- **The layer itself fades slowly.** Over an EV-HAR fitted at the same time, EV-GBM keeps +0.012 to +0.015 for four
  months. The late ages rest on few months, and October is weak for every fit, even a fresh one.
- **Two consequences.** Releases should refit regularly. And a comparison of a fresh model with an older opponent
  measures freshness as well as skill, so a judged comparison needs opponents fitted on the same data.

## What release r1.5 ships
`laplace-engine refit --cutoff 2026-10-27T00:00 --suffix r15` fits, as the rolling test fits a month that starts at
the cutoff:
- **The fit.** GARCH, HAR, EV-HAR and EV-GBM on every hour of a fresh panel from 2025-02-01 to 2026-09-27.
- **The stop.** EV-GBM stopped on 2026-09-27 to 2026-10-27, at 89, 116 and 66 rounds for 1, 4 and 24 hours.
- **The programs.** `garch-r15`, `har-r15`, `ev-har-r15` and `ev-gbm-r15`, whose trees are a 1.5 MB parameter file.

Each new program runs in the sandbox on the stopping month and scores exactly as research does (to five decimals).
Rebuilding gives byte-identical files. On that month, which the trees stopped on, EV-GBM scores +0.0059 per
question over EV-HAR at all horizons: a check, not evidence.

## Caveats
- **The same seven months** as the learned-layer note, overlapping PoC 2's validation and holdout: development
  evidence. The live record is the test.
- **One configuration.** The settings were fixed before the test and not tuned. The inputs are EV-NET's, chosen
  before the rolling test.
- **The program and research.** EV-HAR's program differs slightly from research where its 720-hour mean covers the
  window's 719 hours. Given the same spread, EV-GBM's program is research's exactly; where the spreads differ, a tree
  can take the other branch.
